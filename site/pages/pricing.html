Questions people ask first

Why would more than one account be the paid line?

One account costs a little storage and a sync process. Several accounts is a different shape of workload — and usually a business using it, not a person catching up on a family group.

When does pricing land?

When the service is stable enough to promise something. Until then the honest answer is: unknown, and free.

Would I lose data if I ever stopped paying?

No. A downgrade would drop you to free limits; the store stays until you delete it, and you can export it at any point. Cancelling will never delete messages behind your back.

Is my WhatsApp account at risk?

Linking a device is the same mechanism WhatsApp Web uses. It is still a device you are responsible for — see Security for the honest version, including what automated sending can cost you.
